Expression lines

Frown lines, forehead lines and crow’s-feet that stay when your face rests.

Talk to a clinician

Why this shows up.

Each frown or raised brow folds the skin in the same place. As collagen thins, the crease stops letting go. Movement is the cause, and skin quality decides how much of it shows.

What helps at home.

Daily SPF

Sun breaks down collagen faster than movement does. Wear SPF 30 or higher every morning.

A retinoid at night

A low-strength retinoid supports collagen. Start twice a week and build slowly.

Sleep on your back

Side sleeping presses the same lines into the skin night after night.
