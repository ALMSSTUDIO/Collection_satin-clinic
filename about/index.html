Why we started

Iris founded Satin after watching patients leave a consultation with a shopping list and no plan.

One page

Every first visit ends with one written page: what we recommend, what we would leave alone and what it costs. It is yours to keep, whether or not you book.

The problem

Clinics sell before they look.

A menu makes it easy to buy what you came in for. It cannot tell you whether your skin needs it, or how it will look in a year.

The first visit

So we took the menu away.

You get an examination, a written plan and a price. The $75 is credited to any treatment within 60 days.

Every visit after

The same person checks the result.

The clinician who wrote your plan treats you and reviews it with you. If something is not working, the plan changes in writing.

Medical director
