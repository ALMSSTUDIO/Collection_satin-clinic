Overview

What it is,

plainly.

Laser hair removal targets the pigment in the hair follicle and reduces regrowth over a course of sessions.

We choose the settings for your skin tone and hair colour.

Good to know

Temporary redness is common. Rarely, burns or changes in skin colour can occur, especially on tanned skin. This is why we patch test and ask about sun exposure.
